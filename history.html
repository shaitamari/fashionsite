Give yourself a past

Some of the more interesting journeys need history — a swimsuit bought last May, a serum you reorder every six weeks. A profile created ten minutes ago has neither, so this page writes some.

It writes real purchase records with real dates onto your profile, exactly as a checkout would. Nothing is faked at the point of use: the journeys read the same data whether it arrived from here or from an order placed a year ago.

Pick a shape

Or build your own

Any product, any point in the past. Buy the same thing more than once and the interval between the purchases becomes the one we expect next time.

Now go and look. Open your profile in User Profiles and you will find the purchases array and the next due date. Then open the replenishment or anniversary journey and preview the email — the tokens resolve against this history, so you will see your own products in it.
