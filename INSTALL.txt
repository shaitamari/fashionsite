Fix the last "Preferred Category: Makeup" leak on fresh telco/finance profiles.
Cause: lmn.views (category-view counts that drive preferred_category) was browser-wide,
so beauty/fashion browsing left "Makeup" in it, and it bled onto a fresh telco profile.
Fix: lmn.views is now PER-UUID (like purchases/bookings) and legacy browser-wide views are
dropped, not migrated. Plus telco default_category=Plans, banking=Accounts as a belt-and-braces fallback.

From the posh folder:
  unzip -o ~/Downloads/posh-2026-09-17-views.zip -d /tmp/x && cp -r /tmp/x/posh-2026-09-17-views/* . && python3 build.py --all && posh "Per-uuid category views; telco/banking default_category — kill the Makeup leak"

Test: New visitor on telco -> subscribe -> profile Preferred Category should be Plans (or empty), never Makeup.
