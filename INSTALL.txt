"My relationship" account section (past + future) + delivery/pickup fulfilment.

THE ACCOUNT PAGE, PER VERTICAL — a reusable "your relationship, back and ahead" section:
- Grocery: "Your usual" (purchase history = reorderable staples) + "Your next delivery"
- Commerce (fashion/electronics/home): "Your purchases" + "On its way / To collect"
- Travel: "Past trips" + "Upcoming trips" (bookings array, split by travel_date)
- Telco: "Your plans & add-ons"  ·  Finance: "Your accounts"
Reads the per-uuid arrays site-side (a first-party read, exactly as a real account page reads the
company's own system of record). The SAME arrays sit on the profile for the CDP to segment/message on.

FULFILMENT AT CHECKOUT (config-gated per vertical, verticals.json <v>.fulfilment):
- "Deliver to me" (slot) OR "Collect in store" (store). Grocery = delivery slots; commerce = BOPIS.
- The choice rides on every purchase line (fulfilment/where/eta); the "On its way / To collect" list reads it.
- The forward/delivery status is also the data behind the transactional "your order is on the way" beat.
