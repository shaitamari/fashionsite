Membership & rewards

Three tiers. You move up on your own; nothing to sign up for twice.

Account

How you get it: Any registered business.

What it includes: Online ordering, volume price breaks, thirty-day invoicing after credit approval.

Preferred

How you get it: Over €25,000 a year.

What it includes: A named representative, priority allocation on constrained stock, quarterly pricing review.

Key Account

How you get it: Over €100,000 a year or a framework agreement.

What it includes: Contract pricing, consignment stock options, dedicated application engineering support.

Your tier is shown on every page once you're signed in, and our assistant can tell you how close you are to the next one.
