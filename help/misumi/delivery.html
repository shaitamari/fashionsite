Ordering, shipping & quotes

Timings, costs and what happens when plans change.

Stocked items
Ships the same day on orders placed before 3pm local time. Singapore and Johor next working day; Bangkok and Ho Chi Minh City in two to three.
Made-to-order and configured parts
Lead time is shown on each product page and confirmed on the order acknowledgement. Most configured parts ship in five working days.
Volume pricing
Quantity breaks apply automatically at 10, 50 and 200 units. For larger runs or annual contracts, request a quote and we reply within one working day.
Returns
Stocked items can be returned within fourteen days, unused, in original packaging. Configured parts are non-returnable.
Account terms
Account customers are invoiced monthly on thirty-day terms. Preferred and Key Account tiers have a named representative and priority allocation on constrained stock.
