Membership & rewards

Three tiers. You move up on your own; nothing to sign up for twice.

Member

How you get it: Free — everyone who creates an account.

What it includes: Order history, saved preferences, birthday offer, early sale access by email.

Silver

How you get it: Reached automatically after three orders in a year.

What it includes: Free standard delivery on every order, a dedicated line to our assistant, and previews a day before everyone else.

Gold

How you get it: Reached after ten orders or €1,500 in a year.

What it includes: Free next-day delivery, free returns collection from home, invitations to in-store events, and a personal advisor who knows what you like.

Your tier is shown on every page once you're signed in, and our assistant can tell you how close you are to the next one.
