Ashford Lane — everything a customer asks

Womenswear, menswear, shoes and accessories. This page covers Ashford Lane's delivery & returns, questions, membership tiers, stores and story. Every answer below is about Ashford Lane only.

Ashford Lane: Delivery & returns

Ashford Lane: Standard delivery
Ashford Lane. Free on orders over €75, otherwise €4.95. Arrives in 2–4 working days across the UK and EU.
Ashford Lane: Next-day delivery
Ashford Lane. €9.95, order before 8pm. Not available to the Channel Islands or the Canaries.
Ashford Lane: Click & collect
Ashford Lane. Free, ready in two hours during store opening times. We hold collections for seven days.
Ashford Lane: Returns
Ashford Lane. Thirty days from delivery, any reason, free returns label in the parcel. Refunds reach your card within five working days of the parcel arriving with us.
Ashford Lane: Exchanges
Ashford Lane. Exchange in any store on the spot, or by post using the same label — mark the form 'exchange' and tell us the size or colour.

Ashford Lane: Frequently asked questions

Ashford Lane: Can I change or cancel an order?
Ashford Lane. Within an hour of placing it, yes — go to Orders in your account and choose Cancel. After that it's on its way, and returns are free anyway.
Ashford Lane: Do you ship internationally?
Ashford Lane. To the UK, all EU countries, Switzerland and Norway. Not yet to the US or Australia.
Ashford Lane: How do I track my order?
Ashford Lane. The dispatch email carries a tracking link, and Orders in your account shows the same thing. Ask our assistant and it will tell you where the parcel is.
Ashford Lane: What payment methods do you accept?
Ashford Lane. Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal. Gift cards can be combined with any of them.
Ashford Lane: Is my size out of stock for good?
Ashford Lane. Rarely. Tap Notify me on the product page and we'll message you the moment it's back — most sizes return within three weeks.
Ashford Lane: Do you price-match?
Ashford Lane. No, but items bought in the fourteen days before a price drop are refunded the difference automatically.

Ashford Lane: Membership and rewards

Ashford Lane Member tier
Ashford Lane Member — how you get it: Free — everyone who creates an account. What it includes: Order history, saved preferences, birthday offer, early sale access by email.
Ashford Lane Silver tier
Ashford Lane Silver — how you get it: Reached automatically after three orders in a year. What it includes: Free standard delivery on every order, a dedicated line to our assistant, and previews a day before everyone else.
Ashford Lane Gold tier
Ashford Lane Gold — how you get it: Reached after ten orders or €1,500 in a year. What it includes: Free next-day delivery, free returns collection from home, invitations to in-store events, and a personal advisor who knows what you like.

About Ashford Lane

Ashford Lane started in 2011 as a single shop on Regent Street selling well-made everyday clothes at honest prices. Fifteen years later there are three stores and a site that ships across Europe, but the idea hasn't moved: clothes you'll wear for years, cut properly, priced so you don't have to think twice.

We design everything ourselves in London and make in small runs with the same factories we've worked with since the start — mostly in Portugal and Italy. Nothing is made to be replaced next season.
