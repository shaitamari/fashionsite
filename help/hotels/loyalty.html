Membership & rewards

Three tiers. You move up on your own; nothing to sign up for twice.

Member

How you get it: Free — everyone who creates an account.

What it includes: Member rates, saved preferences, points on every stay or flight.

Silver

How you get it: After two stays or four flights in a year.

What it includes: Late check-out or free seat selection, priority support, and a welcome we get right.

Gold

How you get it: After five stays or ten flights in a year.

What it includes: Free breakfast or lounge access, upgrades where available, and the room or seat you prefer, every time.

Your tier is shown on every page once you're signed in, and our assistant can tell you how close you are to the next one.
