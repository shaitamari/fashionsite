Wayfarer — everything a customer asks

Places worth staying. This page covers Wayfarer's bookings & changes, questions, membership tiers, properties and story. Every answer below is about Wayfarer only.

Wayfarer: Bookings & changes

Wayfarer: Changing a booking
Wayfarer. Dates and rooms can be changed free of charge up to 48 hours before arrival. Inside 48 hours, the first night is charged.
Wayfarer: Cancellation
Wayfarer. Flexible rates cancel free until 6pm local time the day before. Saver rates are non-refundable but can be moved once, within twelve months.
Wayfarer: Check-in and check-out
Wayfarer. Check-in from 3pm, check-out by 11am. Early check-in and late check-out are free for Gold members where the room is available.
Wayfarer: Payment
Wayfarer. Nothing is taken at booking on Flexible rates; we charge on arrival. Saver rates are charged in full when you book.
Wayfarer: Group bookings
Wayfarer. Six rooms or more, or any event, go through our groups desk — email groups@ the brand domain and we reply the same working day.

Wayfarer: Frequently asked questions

Wayfarer: Can I add breakfast after booking?
Wayfarer. Yes, in Manage booking or by asking the concierge. It's €22 a person a day, and free for Gold members.
Wayfarer: Do you allow pets?
Wayfarer. Dogs under 15kg in selected rooms at every property except Casa Luz Kyoto. €25 a night, and we'll have a bed and bowls waiting.
Wayfarer: Is parking available?
Wayfarer. Valet parking at every property, €30 a night. The Grand Lisbon also has a self-park garage two streets away at €18.
Wayfarer: How do I use my points?
Wayfarer. Points can be redeemed against any stay from 5,000 points, or converted to airline miles with our partners at 1:1.
Wayfarer: What's your Wi-Fi like?
Wayfarer. Free, fast and everywhere, including the pool. No login screen.
Wayfarer: Can I request a specific room?
Wayfarer. Tell us your preference — high floor, quiet, sea view — and it's saved to your profile. We honour it whenever the room exists.

Wayfarer: Membership and rewards

Wayfarer Member tier
Wayfarer Member — how you get it: Free — everyone who creates an account. What it includes: Member rates, saved preferences, points on every stay or flight.
Wayfarer Silver tier
Wayfarer Silver — how you get it: After two stays or four flights in a year. What it includes: Late check-out or free seat selection, priority support, and a welcome we get right.
Wayfarer Gold tier
Wayfarer Gold — how you get it: After five stays or ten flights in a year. What it includes: Free breakfast or lounge access, upgrades where available, and the room or seat you prefer, every time.

About Wayfarer

Wayfarer runs three hotels — The Grand Lisbon, Casa Luz Kyoto and Riverside Lodge Amalfi — each different, each looked after as if it were the only one.

We don't do loyalty tiers you have to work for: stay twice and you're Silver, five times and you're Gold, with late check-out, breakfast and the room you prefer.
