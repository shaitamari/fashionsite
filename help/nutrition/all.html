Verdant — everything a customer asks

Supplements, superfoods and snacks. This page covers Verdant's delivery & returns, questions, membership tiers, contact and story. Every answer below is about Verdant only.

Verdant: Delivery & returns

Verdant: Standard delivery
Verdant. Free on orders over €75, otherwise €4.95. Arrives in 2–4 working days across the UK and EU.
Verdant: Next-day delivery
Verdant. €9.95, order before 8pm. Not available to the Channel Islands or the Canaries.
Verdant: Click & collect
Verdant. Free, ready in two hours during store opening times. We hold collections for seven days.
Verdant: Returns
Verdant. Thirty days from delivery, any reason, free returns label in the parcel. Refunds reach your card within five working days of the parcel arriving with us.
Verdant: Exchanges
Verdant. Exchange in any store on the spot, or by post using the same label — mark the form 'exchange' and tell us the size or colour.

Verdant: Frequently asked questions

Verdant: Can I change or cancel an order?
Verdant. Within an hour of placing it, yes — go to Orders in your account and choose Cancel. After that it's on its way, and returns are free anyway.
Verdant: Do you ship internationally?
Verdant. To the UK, all EU countries, Switzerland and Norway. Not yet to the US or Australia.
Verdant: How do I track my order?
Verdant. The dispatch email carries a tracking link, and Orders in your account shows the same thing. Ask our assistant and it will tell you where the parcel is.
Verdant: What payment methods do you accept?
Verdant. Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal. Gift cards can be combined with any of them.
Verdant: Is my size out of stock for good?
Verdant. Rarely. Tap Notify me on the product page and we'll message you the moment it's back — most sizes return within three weeks.
Verdant: Do you price-match?
Verdant. No, but items bought in the fourteen days before a price drop are refunded the difference automatically.

Verdant Subscriber tier

Verdant Subscriber Plus tier
Verdant Subscriber Plus — how you get it: Two or more active subscriptions. What it includes: 20% off, a free product every quarter, and a coach check-in every month.
