Meridian Air — everything a customer asks

Direct routes, fifteen cities. This page covers Meridian Air's bookings & changes, questions, membership tiers, routes & lounges and story. Every answer below is about Meridian Air only.

Meridian Air: Bookings & changes

Meridian Air: Changing a booking
Meridian Air. Dates and rooms can be changed free of charge up to 48 hours before arrival. Inside 48 hours, the first night is charged.
Meridian Air: Cancellation
Meridian Air. Flexible rates cancel free until 6pm local time the day before. Saver rates are non-refundable but can be moved once, within twelve months.
Meridian Air: Check-in and check-out
Meridian Air. Check-in from 3pm, check-out by 11am. Early check-in and late check-out are free for Gold members where the room is available.
Meridian Air: Payment
Meridian Air. Nothing is taken at booking on Flexible rates; we charge on arrival. Saver rates are charged in full when you book.
Meridian Air: Group bookings
Meridian Air. Six rooms or more, or any event, go through our groups desk — email groups@ the brand domain and we reply the same working day.

Meridian Air: Frequently asked questions

Meridian Air: Can I add breakfast after booking?
Meridian Air. Yes, in Manage booking or by asking the concierge. It's €22 a person a day, and free for Gold members.
Meridian Air: Do you allow pets?
Meridian Air. Dogs under 15kg in selected rooms at every property except Casa Luz Kyoto. €25 a night, and we'll have a bed and bowls waiting.
Meridian Air: Is parking available?
Meridian Air. Valet parking at every property, €30 a night. The Grand Lisbon also has a self-park garage two streets away at €18.
Meridian Air: How do I use my points?
Meridian Air. Points can be redeemed against any stay from 5,000 points, or converted to airline miles with our partners at 1:1.
Meridian Air: What's your Wi-Fi like?
Meridian Air. Free, fast and everywhere, including the pool. No login screen.
Meridian Air: Can I request a specific room?
Meridian Air. Tell us your preference — high floor, quiet, sea view — and it's saved to your profile. We honour it whenever the room exists.

Meridian Air: Membership and rewards

Meridian Air Blue tier
Meridian Air Blue — how you get it: Free — everyone who creates an account. What it includes: Member rates, saved preferences, points on every stay or flight.
Meridian Air Silver tier
Meridian Air Silver — how you get it: After two stays or four flights in a year. What it includes: Late check-out or free seat selection, priority support, and a welcome we get right.
Meridian Air Gold tier
Meridian Air Gold — how you get it: After five stays or ten flights in a year. What it includes: Free breakfast or lounge access, upgrades where available, and the room or seat you prefer, every time.

About Meridian Air

Meridian Air flies three routes and flies them well: London–New York, Paris–Tokyo and Rome–Marrakesh. New aircraft, a crew who stay with the airline, and fares that say what they include.

Blue, Silver and Gold members earn miles on every fare and use them on any seat, with no blackout dates.
