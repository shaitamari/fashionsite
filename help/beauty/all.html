Lumen — everything a customer asks

Makeup, Skincare and Fragrance. This page covers Lumen's delivery & returns, questions, membership tiers, stores and story. Every answer below is about Lumen only.

Lumen: Delivery & returns

Lumen: Standard delivery
Lumen. Free on orders over €75, otherwise €4.95. Arrives in 2–4 working days across the UK and EU.
Lumen: Next-day delivery
Lumen. €9.95, order before 8pm. Not available to the Channel Islands or the Canaries.
Lumen: Click & collect
Lumen. Free, ready in two hours during store opening times. We hold collections for seven days.
Lumen: Returns
Lumen. Thirty days from delivery, any reason, free returns label in the parcel. Refunds reach your card within five working days of the parcel arriving with us.
Lumen: Exchanges
Lumen. Exchange in any store on the spot, or by post using the same label — mark the form 'exchange' and tell us the size or colour.

Lumen: Frequently asked questions

Lumen: Can I change or cancel an order?
Lumen. Within an hour of placing it, yes — go to Orders in your account and choose Cancel. After that it's on its way, and returns are free anyway.
Lumen: Do you ship internationally?
Lumen. To the UK, all EU countries, Switzerland and Norway. Not yet to the US or Australia.
Lumen: How do I track my order?
Lumen. The dispatch email carries a tracking link, and Orders in your account shows the same thing. Ask our assistant and it will tell you where the parcel is.
Lumen: What payment methods do you accept?
Lumen. Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal. Gift cards can be combined with any of them.
Lumen: Is my size out of stock for good?
Lumen. Rarely. Tap Notify me on the product page and we'll message you the moment it's back — most sizes return within three weeks.
Lumen: Do you price-match?
Lumen. No, but items bought in the fourteen days before a price drop are refunded the difference automatically.

Lumen: Membership and rewards

Lumen Member tier
Lumen Member — how you get it: Free — everyone who creates an account. What it includes: Order history, saved preferences, birthday offer, early sale access by email.
Lumen Insider tier
Lumen Insider — how you get it: Reached automatically after three orders in a year. What it includes: Free standard delivery on every order, a dedicated line to our assistant, and previews a day before everyone else.
Lumen VIP tier
Lumen VIP — how you get it: Reached after ten orders or €1,500 in a year. What it includes: Free next-day delivery, free returns collection from home, invitations to in-store events, and a personal advisor who knows what you like.

About Lumen

Lumen is a beauty retailer with a point of view: fewer, better products, chosen by people who use them. We opened on Oxford Street in 2016 and now run three stores and this site.

Every product we stock passes a two-week test by our own advisors before it goes on sale, and we drop anything that doesn't hold up. Our consultations are free, unhurried and never end in a hard sell.
