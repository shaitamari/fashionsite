Everyday banking, cards and lending. This page covers Northbank's applications & timings, questions, membership tiers, contact and story. Every answer below is about Northbank only.

Northbank: Applications & timings

Northbank: Opening an account
Northbank. Online in about eight minutes with a photo ID and a selfie. Most accounts are open the same day; some need a manual check, which takes up to two working days.
Northbank: Applying for credit
Northbank. A decision in principle within minutes, with no mark on your credit file. A full application takes one working day.
Northbank: Mortgages
Northbank. An agreement in principle the same day. A full offer typically takes ten working days once we have your documents and the valuation.
Northbank: Switching to us
Northbank. The Current Account Switch Service moves everything across in seven working days, guaranteed. We handle the old bank.
Northbank: Closing an account
Northbank. Any time, from the app, with any balance returned within three working days.

Northbank: Frequently asked questions

Northbank: Is my money protected?
Northbank. Deposits are protected up to €100,000 per person under the deposit guarantee scheme.
Northbank: How do I report a lost card?
Northbank. Freeze it in the app in one tap, then order a replacement — it arrives in two working days and works in your wallet immediately.
Northbank: Do you charge for using my card abroad?
Northbank. No fees on card payments in any currency. Cash withdrawals abroad are free up to €200 a month, then 2%.
Northbank: Can I book a call with an advisor?
Northbank. Yes — ask the assistant or use Book a call in the app. Slots are available weekdays 8am–8pm and Saturdays until 2pm.
Northbank: What do the tiers include?
Northbank. Everyday is free. Plus adds travel insurance and fee-free foreign cash for €9 a month. Premier adds a dedicated advisor and airport lounge access for €25 a month.
Northbank: How do I make a complaint?
Northbank. In the app under Help, or in writing. We acknowledge within two working days and resolve most cases within fifteen.

Northbank: Membership and rewards

Northbank Everyday tier

Northbank Plus tier
Northbank Plus — how you get it: A monthly fee with real benefits. What it includes: Travel cover, higher limits, fee-free cash abroad, faster support.
Northbank Premier tier
Northbank Premier — how you get it: For people who want a named person. What it includes: A dedicated advisor, priority claims or lending decisions, and lounge access where it applies.
