Membership & rewards

Three tiers. You move up on your own; nothing to sign up for twice.

Pay As You Go

How you get it: Pay as you go — no contract.

What it includes: Top up when you like; allowances never expire while the SIM is active.

Monthly

How you get it: Monthly — one bill, more for the money.

What it includes: 5G, EU roaming included, handset finance available.

Business

How you get it: Business — for teams.

What it includes: Shared data across up to 20 SIMs, one bill, a named account manager.

Your tier is shown on every page once you're signed in, and our assistant can tell you how close you are to the next one.
