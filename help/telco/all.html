Vantis — everything a customer asks

Phones, plans and accessories. This page covers Vantis's plans, top-ups & switching, questions, membership tiers, stores and story. Every answer below is about Vantis only.

Vantis: Plans, top-ups & switching

Vantis: Changing plan
Vantis. Move up any time; the new plan starts immediately and we pro-rate the month. Move down at your next billing date.
Vantis: Top-ups
Vantis. From €5 to €50, instant, from the app or any Vantis store. Top-ups never expire while the SIM is active.
Vantis: Keeping your number
Vantis. Text PAC to 65075 on your old network, give us the code, and your number moves within one working day.
Vantis: Roaming
Vantis. EU roaming is included on every plan at your home allowance. Outside the EU, a Travel Pass is €5 a day for 2GB.
Vantis: Cancelling
Vantis. Pay-as-you-go: just stop. Monthly: thirty days' notice, no exit fee after the minimum term.

Vantis: Frequently asked questions

Vantis: What's included in Vantis Unlimited?
Vantis. Unlimited data, calls and texts in the EU, 5G where available, and two Travel Pass days a month free.
Vantis: Why is my data slow?
Vantis. Check you're on 5G in Settings; if you're on Vantis 5, the plan is capped at 10Mbps after 5GB. Vantis 20 and Unlimited are uncapped.
Vantis: Can I get a new phone on my plan?
Vantis. Yes — any handset can be added to a Monthly plan over 24 or 36 months, with no upfront cost on most models.
Vantis: How do I check my usage?
Vantis. The app shows it live. Or ask the assistant — it can see your plan and your remaining allowance.
Vantis: Do you cover my area?
Vantis. 99% 4G and 85% 5G coverage nationally. Enter a postcode on the Coverage page for street-level detail.
Vantis: What is Vantis Business?
Vantis. Shared data across up to 20 SIMs, one bill, a named account manager and priority support.

Vantis: Membership and rewards

Vantis Pay As You Go tier
Vantis Pay As You Go — how you get it: Pay as you go — no contract. What it includes: Top up when you like; allowances never expire while the SIM is active.
Vantis Monthly tier
Vantis Monthly — how you get it: Monthly — one bill, more for the money. What it includes: 5G, EU roaming included, handset finance available.
Vantis Business tier
Vantis Business — how you get it: Business — for teams. What it includes: Shared data across up to 20 SIMs, one bill, a named account manager.
