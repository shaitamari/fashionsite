Kestrel — everything a customer asks

Home appliances and technology. This page covers Kestrel's delivery & returns, questions, membership tiers, stores and story. Every answer below is about Kestrel only.

Kestrel: Delivery & returns

Kestrel: Standard delivery
Kestrel. Free on orders over €75, otherwise €4.95. Arrives in 2–4 working days across the UK and EU.
Kestrel: Next-day delivery
Kestrel. €9.95, order before 8pm. Not available to the Channel Islands or the Canaries.
Kestrel: Click & collect
Kestrel. Free, ready in two hours during store opening times. We hold collections for seven days.
Kestrel: Returns
Kestrel. Thirty days from delivery, any reason, free returns label in the parcel. Refunds reach your card within five working days of the parcel arriving with us.
Kestrel: Exchanges
Kestrel. Exchange in any store on the spot, or by post using the same label — mark the form 'exchange' and tell us the size or colour.

Kestrel: Frequently asked questions

Kestrel: Can I change or cancel an order?
Kestrel. Within an hour of placing it, yes — go to Orders in your account and choose Cancel. After that it's on its way, and returns are free anyway.
Kestrel: Do you ship internationally?
Kestrel. To the UK, all EU countries, Switzerland and Norway. Not yet to the US or Australia.
Kestrel: How do I track my order?
Kestrel. The dispatch email carries a tracking link, and Orders in your account shows the same thing. Ask our assistant and it will tell you where the parcel is.
Kestrel: What payment methods do you accept?
Kestrel. Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal. Gift cards can be combined with any of them.
Kestrel: Is my size out of stock for good?
Kestrel. Rarely. Tap Notify me on the product page and we'll message you the moment it's back — most sizes return within three weeks.
Kestrel: Do you price-match?
Kestrel. No, but items bought in the fourteen days before a price drop are refunded the difference automatically.

Kestrel: Membership and rewards

Kestrel Member tier
Kestrel Member — how you get it: Free — everyone who creates an account. What it includes: Order history, saved preferences, birthday offer, early sale access by email.
Kestrel Plus tier
Kestrel Plus — how you get it: Reached automatically after three orders in a year. What it includes: Free standard delivery on every order, a dedicated line to our assistant, and previews a day before everyone else.
Kestrel Pro tier
Kestrel Pro — how you get it: Reached after ten orders or €1,500 in a year. What it includes: Free next-day delivery, free returns collection from home, invitations to in-store events, and a personal advisor who knows what you like.

About Kestrel

Our advisors will tell you when something isn't worth the money, and our installation team will set it up properly. Two-year warranty on everything, no registration required.
