Harvest Row — everything a customer asks

Food, drink and household. This page covers Harvest Row's delivery & returns, questions, membership tiers, stores and story. Every answer below is about Harvest Row only.

Harvest Row: Delivery & returns

Harvest Row: Standard delivery
Harvest Row. Free on orders over €75, otherwise €4.95. Arrives in 2–4 working days across the UK and EU.
Harvest Row: Next-day delivery
Harvest Row. €9.95, order before 8pm. Not available to the Channel Islands or the Canaries.
Harvest Row: Click & collect
Harvest Row. Free, ready in two hours during store opening times. We hold collections for seven days.
Harvest Row: Returns
Harvest Row. Thirty days from delivery, any reason, free returns label in the parcel. Refunds reach your card within five working days of the parcel arriving with us.
Harvest Row: Exchanges
Harvest Row. Exchange in any store on the spot, or by post using the same label — mark the form 'exchange' and tell us the size or colour.

Harvest Row: Frequently asked questions

Harvest Row: Can I change or cancel an order?
Harvest Row. Within an hour of placing it, yes — go to Orders in your account and choose Cancel. After that it's on its way, and returns are free anyway.
Harvest Row: Do you ship internationally?
Harvest Row. To the UK, all EU countries, Switzerland and Norway. Not yet to the US or Australia.
Harvest Row: How do I track my order?
Harvest Row. The dispatch email carries a tracking link, and Orders in your account shows the same thing. Ask our assistant and it will tell you where the parcel is.
Harvest Row: What payment methods do you accept?
Harvest Row. Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal. Gift cards can be combined with any of them.
Harvest Row: Is my size out of stock for good?
Harvest Row. Rarely. Tap Notify me on the product page and we'll message you the moment it's back — most sizes return within three weeks.
Harvest Row: Do you price-match?
Harvest Row. No, but items bought in the fourteen days before a price drop are refunded the difference automatically.

Harvest Row: Membership and rewards

Harvest Row Member tier
Harvest Row Member — how you get it: Free — everyone who creates an account. What it includes: Order history, saved preferences, birthday offer, early sale access by email.
Harvest Row Plus tier
Harvest Row Plus — how you get it: Reached automatically after three orders in a year. What it includes: Free standard delivery on every order, a dedicated line to our assistant, and previews a day before everyone else.
Harvest Row Plus Annual tier
Harvest Row Plus Annual — how you get it: Reached after ten orders or €1,500 in a year. What it includes: Free next-day delivery, free returns collection from home, invitations to in-store events, and a personal advisor who knows what you like.

About Harvest Row

Harvest Row is a neighbourhood grocer with three stores in north London and delivery across the city. Fresh food from named farms, a good wine wall, and the everyday things at fair prices.

We deliver in one-hour slots from 7am to 10pm in electric vans, and our pickers will call you about substitutions rather than guessing.
