Delivery & returns

Timings, costs and what happens when plans change.

Standard delivery
Free on orders over €75, otherwise €4.95. Arrives in 2–4 working days across the UK and EU.
Next-day delivery
€9.95, order before 8pm. Not available to the Channel Islands or the Canaries.
Click & collect
Free, ready in two hours during store opening times. We hold collections for seven days.
Returns
Thirty days from delivery, any reason, free returns label in the parcel. Refunds reach your card within five working days of the parcel arriving with us.
Exchanges
Exchange in any store on the spot, or by post using the same label — mark the form 'exchange' and tell us the size or colour.
