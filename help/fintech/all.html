Loop — everything a customer asks

Money, moved properly. This page covers Loop's applications & timings, questions, membership tiers, contact and story. Every answer below is about Loop only.

Loop: Applications & timings

Loop: Opening an account
Loop. Online in about eight minutes with a photo ID and a selfie. Most accounts are open the same day; some need a manual check, which takes up to two working days.
Loop: Applying for credit
Loop. A decision in principle within minutes, with no mark on your credit file. A full application takes one working day.
Loop: Mortgages
Loop. An agreement in principle the same day. A full offer typically takes ten working days once we have your documents and the valuation.
Loop: Switching to us
Loop. The Current Account Switch Service moves everything across in seven working days, guaranteed. We handle the old bank.
Loop: Closing an account
Loop. Any time, from the app, with any balance returned within three working days.

Loop: Frequently asked questions

Loop: Is my money protected?
Loop. Deposits are protected up to €100,000 per person under the deposit guarantee scheme.
Loop: How do I report a lost card?
Loop. Freeze it in the app in one tap, then order a replacement — it arrives in two working days and works in your wallet immediately.
Loop: Do you charge for using my card abroad?
Loop. No fees on card payments in any currency. Cash withdrawals abroad are free up to €200 a month, then 2%.
Loop: Can I book a call with an advisor?
Loop. Yes — ask the assistant or use Book a call in the app. Slots are available weekdays 8am–8pm and Saturdays until 2pm.
Loop: What do the tiers include?
Loop. Everyday is free. Plus adds travel insurance and fee-free foreign cash for €9 a month. Premier adds a dedicated advisor and airport lounge access for €25 a month.
Loop: How do I make a complaint?
Loop. In the app under Help, or in writing. We acknowledge within two working days and resolve most cases within fifteen.

Loop: Membership and rewards

Loop Free tier
Loop Free — how you get it: The standard account or cover, free of monthly fees. What it includes: Everything you need to start, with no fee you weren't told about.
Loop Plus tier
Loop Plus — how you get it: A monthly fee with real benefits. What it includes: Travel cover, higher limits, fee-free cash abroad, faster support.
Loop Premium tier
Loop Premium — how you get it: For people who want a named person. What it includes: A dedicated advisor, priority claims or lending decisions, and lounge access where it applies.
