Canon — everything a customer asks

Cameras, lenses, printers and everything they need. This page covers Canon's delivery & returns, questions, membership tiers, support & contact and story. Every answer below is about Canon only.

Canon: Delivery & returns

Canon: Standard delivery
Canon. Free on orders over €75, otherwise €4.95. Arrives in 2–4 working days across the UK and EU.
Canon: Next-day delivery
Canon. €9.95, order before 8pm. Not available to the Channel Islands or the Canaries.
Canon: Click & collect
Canon. Free, ready in two hours during store opening times. We hold collections for seven days.
Canon: Returns
Canon. Thirty days from delivery, any reason, free returns label in the parcel. Refunds reach your card within five working days of the parcel arriving with us.
Canon: Exchanges
Canon. Exchange in any store on the spot, or by post using the same label — mark the form 'exchange' and tell us the size or colour.

Canon: Frequently asked questions

Canon: Can I change or cancel an order?
Canon. Within an hour of placing it, yes — go to Orders in your account and choose Cancel. After that it's on its way, and returns are free anyway.
Canon: Do you ship internationally?
Canon. To the UK, all EU countries, Switzerland and Norway. Not yet to the US or Australia.
Canon: How do I track my order?
Canon. The dispatch email carries a tracking link, and Orders in your account shows the same thing. Ask our assistant and it will tell you where the parcel is.
Canon: What payment methods do you accept?
Canon. Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal. Gift cards can be combined with any of them.
Canon: Is my size out of stock for good?
Canon. Rarely. Tap Notify me on the product page and we'll message you the moment it's back — most sizes return within three weeks.
Canon: Do you price-match?
Canon. No, but items bought in the fourteen days before a price drop are refunded the difference automatically.

Canon: Membership and rewards

Canon Member tier
Canon Member — how you get it: Free — everyone who creates an account. What it includes: Order history, saved preferences, birthday offer, early sale access by email.
Canon Canon Plus tier
Canon Canon Plus — how you get it: Reached automatically after three orders in a year. What it includes: Free standard delivery on every order, a dedicated line to our assistant, and previews a day before everyone else.
Canon Canon Pro tier
Canon Canon Pro — how you get it: Reached after ten orders or €1,500 in a year. What it includes: Free next-day delivery, free returns collection from home, invitations to in-store events, and a personal advisor who knows what you like.

About Canon

Canon Canada is the Canadian arm of Canon, selling cameras, lenses, printers and the ink, paper and accessories that fit them, direct from shop.canon.ca and through retailers across Canada.

Every product sold on shop.canon.ca is covered by Canon Canada's warranty and supported by Canon Canada's Product Experts, available Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 16:30 Eastern. Extended coverage is available through CarePAK, and ink can be delivered automatically with the Auto Replenishment Service.
